Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CleanTrack collects, uses, stores, and protects information when people use the service. This draft must be completed with the actual operator and deployment details before public launch.

1. Information We May Process

Depending on enabled features, CleanTrack may process:

  • Account details such as name, email address, and optional phone number.
  • Organization, team, role, membership, and invitation information.
  • Tasks, assignments, schedules, completion records, reviews, and audit events.
  • Operational evidence such as uploaded photos, documents, and voice notes.
  • Location, zone, QR verification, and timestamps associated with work activities.
  • Messages, announcements, notification preferences, and support requests.
  • Technical information needed to secure, operate, and troubleshoot the service.

2. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to provide authentication, manage organizations and teams, assign and verify work, maintain operational records, generate reports, send authorized notifications, prevent abuse, and maintain service reliability.

3. Access and Organization Boundaries

Access should be limited according to assigned roles, organization membership, team responsibilities, and record permissions. Organization membership does not by itself authorize access to every private record. Authorized administrators may access certain organizational records to perform their duties.

4. Photos, Files, and Location Data

Uploaded evidence and location-related records may be used to verify operational activities. The production service must restrict access to these records, protect downloads, enforce upload limits, and apply documented retention and deletion rules.

5. Sharing and Service Providers

CleanTrack may rely on hosting, database, email, file-storage, mapping, or other providers when enabled. Before launch, list the actual providers, what information each receives, and any relevant international transfers. Information should not be shared with unrelated parties except as disclosed, directed by the user, or required by law.

6. Security and Retention

Appropriate safeguards should include secure authentication, access controls, encrypted connections, protected file storage, audit records, backups, and recovery procedures. No system can guarantee absolute security. Retention periods must be defined for account records, operational evidence, logs, and backups.

7. Your Choices and Requests

Subject to applicable law and organizational recordkeeping obligations, users should be able to request access to, correction of, export of, or deletion of their personal information. Provide a verified contact method for privacy requests before public launch.

8. Children

CleanTrack is intended for workplace operations, not for use by children. The operator should define and enforce an appropriate minimum age consistent with applicable law.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the service or its data practices change. Material changes should be communicated as required by applicable law.
